    About This Site

  • I will primarily be using this website for documenting research projects related to ancient history that I have worked on, as well as translations of Old Persian inscriptions. Last updated 15th June 2026.

    Primary Interests

  • History of the Achaemenid Empire: all aspects, but specifically Persian Babylonia, Lycian monuments and epigraphy, the Persepolis archives, religious history and acculturation, royal inscriptions, and ideology of kingship. I am especially interested in aspects of interaction between the royal/satrapal administration and local power centres such as temples, and the perspectives that can be offered from taking a comparative approach across different regions of the empire.
  • Related/adjacent areas of the Ancient Near East or Eastern Mediterranean, especially the Neo-Babylonian Empire in the context of the so-called "Long Sixth Century".
  • Study of ancient languages: Old Persian, Akkadian, Imperial Aramaic.
